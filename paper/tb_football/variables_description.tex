%% ── Identifiers ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
year &
Year in which the match took place. \\

stage &
Group identifier (A to H). \\

home\_team &
Name of the home team. \\

away\_team &
Name of the away team. \\

goal\_minute &
Minute of the match in which the goal was scored (denoted $t_k$ in the regression specifications). \\

%% ── Elo ratings ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
elo\_home &
World Football Elo rating of the home team prior to the match, retrieved from \url{https://www.international-football.net/}. \\

elo\_away &
World Football Elo rating of the away team prior to the match. \\

Elo\_diff &
Absolute difference between the two teams' Elo ratings at goal $k$ (match-level competitive imbalance measure used in Spec~1 and Spec~2). \\

Elo\_std &
Standard deviation of Elo ratings across the four teams in the group (group-level competitive balance measure used in Spec~2). \\

%% ── Group standings ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1st &
Team ranked first in the group immediately after the goal. \\

1st\_points &
Number of points held by the first-ranked team after the goal. \\

1st\_goals\_diff &
Overall goal difference of the first-ranked team after the goal. \\

1st\_goals\_scored &
Total goals scored by the first-ranked team after the goal. \\

1st\_last\_game\_goals\_diff &
Match-specific goal difference of the first-ranked team at the current minute. Used in the appendix to infer match points (\texttt{match\_pts}). \\

2nd &
Team ranked second in the group immediately after the goal. \\

2nd\_points &
Number of points held by the second-ranked team after the goal. \\

2nd\_goals\_diff &
Overall goal difference of the second-ranked team after the goal. \\

2nd\_goals\_scored &
Total goals scored by the second-ranked team after the goal. \\

2nd\_last\_game\_goals\_diff &
Match-specific goal difference of the second-ranked team at the current minute. \\

3rd &
Team ranked third in the group immediately after the goal. \\

3rd\_points &
Number of points held by the third-ranked team after the goal. \\

3rd\_goals\_diff &
Overall goal difference of the third-ranked team after the goal. \\

3rd\_goals\_scored &
Total goals scored by the third-ranked team after the goal. \\

3rd\_last\_game\_goals\_diff &
Match-specific goal difference of the third-ranked team at the current minute. \\

4th &
Team ranked fourth in the group immediately after the goal. \\

4th\_points &
Number of points held by the fourth-ranked team after the goal. \\

4th\_goals\_diff &
Overall goal difference of the fourth-ranked team after the goal. \\

4th\_goals\_scored &
Total goals scored by the fourth-ranked team after the goal. \\

4th\_last\_game\_goals\_diff &
Match-specific goal difference of the fourth-ranked team at the current minute. \\

%% ── Suspense logic variables ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
third\_qualify &
Dummy equal to 1 if the third-ranked team qualifies for the next stage (e.g.\ best third-placed formats), 0 if only the top two qualify. Determines which cut-off pair (2nd vs 3rd, or 3rd vs 4th) is evaluated for suspense. \\

pts\_diff &
Points difference between the first non-qualified team (lagging) and the last qualified team (leading) after the goal, denoted $\texttt{pts\_diff}_0$ in the appendix. \\

gls\_diff &
Overall goal difference between the lagging and leading teams after the goal, denoted $\texttt{gls\_diff}_0$ in the appendix. \\

gs\_diff &
Goals scored by the lagging team minus goals scored by the leading team, denoted $\texttt{gs\_diff}_0$ in the appendix. Used as the third tier of the FIFA tie-breaking hierarchy after points and overall goal difference. \\

match\_pts &
Match points inferred from the match goal difference at each minute: equal to \texttt{win\_pts} if match GD $> 0$, 1 if match GD $= 0$, and 0 if match GD $< 0$. \\

win\_pts &
Points awarded for a win: 2 if \texttt{year} $\leq$ 1992, 3 otherwise. \\

h2h &
Dummy equal to 1 if the current match is played between the lagging and leading teams (i.e.\ their head-to-head fixture is ongoing), 0 if they are in separate matches. Affects how one-goal shocks propagate in the suspense calculation. \\

h2h\_suspense &
Dummy equal to 1 for observations where the qualification cut-off between the lagging and leading teams would be decided by the head-to-head tie-break under FIFA rules (i.e.\ both a +1 goal for the lagging team and a +1 concession by the leading team produce a full tie on points, overall goal difference, and overall goals scored). \\

%% ── Outcome and treatment variables ─────────────────────────────────────────
suspense &
Binary indicator equal to 1 if a single goal in any ongoing group match would change the composition of teams qualifying for the knockout stage, 0 otherwise. The main outcome variable. \\

qual\_changed &
Dummy equal to 1 if the set of qualified teams changes as a consequence of the goal, 0 otherwise. \\

qual\_count &
Cumulative count of how many times the composition of qualified teams has changed within the group (resets to 0 at the start of each group's last matchday). \\

FIFA\_rule &
Dummy equal to 1 for observations evaluated under FIFA's goal-difference tie-breaking rule, 0 for observations evaluated under UEFA's head-to-head rule. The main treatment variable in Spec~1 and Spec~2. \\
