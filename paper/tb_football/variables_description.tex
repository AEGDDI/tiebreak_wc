minute & Minute of the match at which the event occurs. \\


score\_home & Number of goals scored by the home team at that minute. \\


score\_away & Number of goals scored by the away team at that minute. \\


P\_home & Model-implied probability of a home win after the event. \\

P\_draw & Model-implied probability of a draw after the event. 


P\_away & Model-implied probability of an away win after the event. \\


dP\_home & Change in the probability of a home win relative to the previous minute. \\


dP\_draw & Change in the probability of a draw relative to the previous minute. \\


dP\_away & Change in the probability of an away win relative to the previous minute. \\


shock & Maximum absolute change among $|dP_\text{home}|$, $|dP_\text{draw}|$, and $|dP_\text{away}|$. \\
surprise & Sum of absolute probability changes: $|dP_\text{home}| + |dP_\text{draw}| + |dP_\text{away}|$. \\


suspense & Weighted uncertainty measure computed as $\lambda \sum_j P_j(1-P_j)$, where $j \in \{\text{home}, \text{draw}, \text{away}\}$. \\


event & Text description of the match event (e.g., start of the match, goal by home or away team). \\

\end{longtable}
